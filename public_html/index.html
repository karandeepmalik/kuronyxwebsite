02 What we are

Some gaps are so familiar they stop looking like gaps.

Kuronyx Speciality Pharmacy exists in that space — India's first veterinary-only compounding and import pharmacy, prescription-only, built for the vets and the animals that clinical infrastructure forgot.

03 Why this matters

In India, a cat can be diagnosed with a treatable disease and still die — not because medicine doesn't exist, but because the right medicine, at the right concentration, for the right species, does not exist here in any regulated form.

Veterinarians know this. They work around it every day. Kuronyx was built so they don't have to.
