02Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • “Data Principal” refers to the individual to whom the personal data relates.
  • “Data Fiduciary” refers to Kuronyx Sciences Private Limited, which alone or in conjunction with other persons determine the purpose and means of processing of personal data.
  • “Personal Data” means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • “Processing” means an automated operation or set of operations performed on digital personal data, and includes operations such as collection, storage, retrieval, use, adaptation, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, restriction, structuring, erasure or destruction.
  • “Consent” means any unconditional, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of the Data Principal's wishes by which they, by a clear affirmative action, signify their agreement to the processing of their personal data for a specified purpose.

03Information we collect

We collect various categories of personal data to provide and improve our services, ensuring that such collection is limited to what is necessary for the stated purposes (data minimisation principle). The information collected includes:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and phone number, primarily collected when you engage with us for enquiries or service requests.
  • Professional information: details such as your job title, the name of your clinic or hospital, and your city, which assists us in understanding your professional needs and tailoring our outreach.
  • Communication data: any messages or content you share with us via our website's contact form or through direct communication channels.
  • Newsletter subscription data: your email address, specifically collected for the purpose of sending you our newsletters, only upon your explicit subscription.
  • Website usage data: non-personally identifiable information related to your interaction with our website, such as pages visited, browser type, device information, and IP address, collected to enhance user experience and website functionality.

04How we use your information

Your personal data is processed strictly for specified, explicit, and lawful purposes, ensuring transparency and accountability. The purposes for which we use your information include:

  • To respond to enquiries: to address and respond to your queries, requests, or feedback submitted via our contact form.
  • To send newsletters: to deliver our periodic newsletters containing updates, insights, and relevant information, exclusively to individuals who have subscribed.
  • To engage with veterinary professionals: to follow up with veterinary professionals who have expressed interest in our services, fostering professional relationships.
  • To improve our website and marketing efforts: to analyse website usage patterns and user preferences, enabling us to enhance our website's functionality, content, and the effectiveness of our marketing strategies.
  • To comply with applicable laws: to fulfil our legal and regulatory obligations under Indian law, including but not limited to the DPDP Act, 2023, and other relevant statutes.
  • For internal record keeping: to maintain accurate and organised records of our interactions and operations, essential for business administration and compliance.

05Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds, as stipulated by the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Consent: we rely on your explicit consent when you submit a form or subscribe to our newsletter. Your consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and you have the right to withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: we process certain data based on our legitimate interests, such as communicating with veterinary professionals who have shown interest in our services, improving our website, and enhancing our marketing efforts, provided that such interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation: we process your data where it is necessary to comply with a legal obligation imposed upon us by Indian law, including responding to lawful requests from public authorities.

06Data security and protection

Kuronyx is committed to implementing robust technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Our security protocols are regularly reviewed and updated to ensure the highest level of data protection.

  • Secure storage: your data is stored on secure servers with restricted access, ensuring that only authorised Kuronyx team members have access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.
  • Access controls: we employ stringent access controls, including multi-factor authentication and role-based access, to limit data access to personnel who require it for their legitimate duties.
  • Data encryption: where appropriate, data is encrypted both in transit and at rest to prevent interception and unauthorised access.
  • Internal policies: we maintain comprehensive internal data protection policies and conduct regular training for our employees to ensure adherence to best practices in data handling and security.
  • No sale or trade: we unequivocally state that we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

07Data retention

Once the purpose is served, or the legal retention period expires, your data will be securely deleted or anonymised. You may request the deletion of your data by contacting us at ops@kuronyx.in.

09Your rights as a data principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you, as a Data Principal, are endowed with several fundamental rights concerning your personal data. Kuronyx is committed to facilitating the exercise of these rights:

  • Right to access: you have the right to obtain confirmation from us as to whether or not personal data concerning you is being processed, and, where that is the case, access to the personal data.
  • Right to correction and erasure: you have the right to request the correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data and the completion of incomplete personal data. You also have the right to request the deletion or erasure of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to grievance redressal: you have the right to have your grievances redressed by a Grievance Officer, as detailed in Section 14 of this policy.
  • Right to nominate: you have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Right to withdraw consent: you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
  • Right to unsubscribe: you can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by following the instructions provided in the newsletter itself.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ops@kuronyx.in. We will respond to your request in accordance with the DPDP Act, 2023.

17GS-441524 requests and veterinary account applications

Where you submit a GS-441524 request, or apply for a veterinary account, we collect additional categories of personal data beyond those described in Section 3, strictly to enable prescription review, verification and fulfilment:

  • Cat owner information: full name, email, phone, and address, collected when a request is submitted on behalf of a patient.
  • Patient (animal) information: name, species, breed, sex, age or date of birth, weight, neutered/spayed status, microchip identifier, and relevant clinical information, collected to support prescription review.
  • Veterinarian and clinic information: name, registration number, registration state, qualification, practice type, and clinic details, collected to verify the treating veterinarian and, for veterinary account applicants, to assess the application.
  • Prescriptions and supporting documents: uploaded prescriptions, laboratory or diagnostic reports, and verification documents (such as registration certificates), collected solely for prescription review, verification and compliance purposes.

This information is used only to review the request or application, communicate with the treating veterinarian or owner, determine and prepare a formulation where appropriate, and maintain the records our compliance obligations require. Prescriptions and supporting documents are stored outside our public web infrastructure and access is restricted to authorised Kuronyx personnel.

This data is retained for as long as necessary to support the case, any follow-up care, and applicable regulatory recordkeeping requirements, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised. You may request access to or deletion of this information, subject to our recordkeeping obligations, by contacting ops@kuronyx.in.
