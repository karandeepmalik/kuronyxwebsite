# Veterinary account approval checklist

Approving an application gives that person a portal login that can submit GS-441524 prescription
requests, and the activation link goes to the email address the applicant typed in. Nothing in the
software proves they are the registered veterinarian they claim to be, so **a staff member must do
the checks below before clicking Approve**, and record what they did in the review note box (it is
saved with the approval in the application's internal notes).

The application form requires a registration certificate upload, so there is always something to
check against.

## Before approving

1. **Open the uploaded registration certificate** (Documents panel). Check that the name, registration
   number, issuing State Veterinary Council and year match what was typed into the form, and that
   the document looks genuine (not edited, not a different person's).
2. **Look the registration up with the issuing council** (State Veterinary Council register, or the
   Veterinary Council of India register where it lists the person). The name and registration number
   on the register must match the application. If the council has no searchable register, email or
   phone the council office to confirm.
3. **Phone callback.** Call the veterinarian on a number you found independently - the clinic's
   listing on Google Maps / the council register / a clinic website - not only the mobile typed on
   the form. Confirm they applied, and confirm the email address the activation link will go to.
4. **Email ownership.** The activation link is only ever sent to the professional email on the
   application. If that is a free-mail address that doesn't match the name or clinic, ask during
   the callback for a clinic-domain address, or accept it only after the person confirms it verbally.
5. **Duplicates.** Search the applications list for the same registration number or email. A second
   application from the same registration number under a different email is a red flag.

## If anything doesn't match

Use **Request More Information** (and the email composer) or **Reject**. Do not approve "to sort
it out later": approval is what unlocks the portal.

## Recording it

Put one line in the Note box when approving, for example:
`Certificate MH-VET-1234 checked against MSVC register 02 Oct; called clinic on 020-xxxxxxx, confirmed.`
The note is appended to the application's internal notes with the date and your name.

## Later problems

If a veterinarian turns out not to be genuine, **Suspend** the application: that also locks the portal
account and ends any open sign-in immediately.
